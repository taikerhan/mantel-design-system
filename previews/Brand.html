Brand

Logo · principles · voice · ship checklist
Logo

Primary navy #0053A7 is applied via SVG fill — never CSS filter.

Principles
01

Refined & minimal

Hairline edges over hard borders. Generous whitespace. Type-driven hierarchy, not chrome-driven.

02

Monochrome navy

Brand-600 carries the surface. Status colors only ever appear for meaning — never for decoration.

03

Numbers right, words left

Every numeric value is tabular. Money, percentages, counts, timestamps — they align in a column so the eye can scan a row.

Voice — do & don't
Do
"Robert & Linda viewed the proposal 4 minutes ago."
Concrete subject. Specific timing. No filler.
Don't
"🎉 Exciting news! Your prospect just engaged with your amazing proposal!"
Emoji. Marketing voice. Empty adjectives.
Do
"Reply now"
Verb + scope. Action-first.
Don't
"Click here to take action"
Vague verb. No specific outcome.
Ship checklist
01
Does it reuse existing tokens (brand, ink, accent) — no one-off hexes?
02
Are status colors used for meaning, never for decoration?
03
Are all cards on the 16px radius / hairline ring / no-border rhythm?
04
Are numbers tabular-nums everywhere they appear in rows?
05
Are buttons rounded-full, with at most one brand-fill per surface?
06
Does the page still feel coherent on mobile (drawer + stacked headers + hidden pills)?
07
Did you avoid emojis in copy (status pills, seed data, comments)?
