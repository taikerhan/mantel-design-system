Example 1 — Single system, no navigator

Most homes have one system, so most proposals never see the navigator. The card is just the equipment list and staging chart — no tabs, no pinned bar, zero added chrome.

Example 2 — Three systems, stacked + scroll navigator

Every system's full equipment and staging chart stay on the page — nothing hides behind a switcher. Past one system, a small numbered tab row pins to the top as a scroll navigator: it tracks the zone under the reading line and jumps on click. Scroll the card to try it.

Edge case — a four-zone home on mobile

Four zones overflow the width, so the pinned bar scrolls sideways (compact 14px tabs, hidden scrollbar, edge fades, arrows for mouse users) while it keeps tracking the page: as you scroll into a zone, its tab auto-centers in the bar. Jumps work the same as on desktop.
