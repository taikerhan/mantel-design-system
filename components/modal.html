Modal

Centered dialog · backdrop fade + scale-in

When to use — Small confirmations & single-field · Default standard forms & settings · Wide two-column forms & previews · Large dense tables & dashboards (caps at 1040, never sprawls) · Full immersive multi-step, and the default on mobile.

Confirm dialog

.mt-modal.is-confirmCompact, icon-led “are you sure?” — layers on the modal shell

Deactivate discount?

“Copy of Sunrise 10% Discount” will be turned off and can’t be applied to new proposals. It will be stored in the ‘Inactive’ tab, and you can reactivate it anytime.

Reuses .mt-backdrop + .mt-modal; .is-confirm swaps to a compact 440px block with a tinted icon, title, body, and a right-aligned .btn-neutral / .btn-destructive pair. The destructive action is the elevated focal button.role="alertdialog"
Do

Use the full shell for settings or short single-step flows; reach for .is-confirm for destructive “are you sure?” prompts. ≤1 primary button in the foot, on the right.

Don't

Stack modals. Don't put scrollable lists or multi-step forms in a modal — switch to a sheet.
