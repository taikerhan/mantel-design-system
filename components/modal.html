Scroll & height

.mt-overlay-bodyBody is the only scroll region — head & foot stay pinned

When the body overflows, only it scrolls. The header drops a hairline + shadow once you scroll past the top (.is-scrolled); the footer lifts a shadow while content still sits below it (.is-scrollable:not(.is-end)) — both clear at the ends of the range. Mobile height tiers — under 640px the modal docks to the bottom as a sheet: default hugs content, .is-half = 55vh, .is-tall = 90vh, .is-full takes the whole screen.

Proposal terms

Review before sending

Scroll the body — the header and footer stay pinned and pick up an edge shadow.

This proposal presents the recommended system configuration for 442 Oak Ridge Dr — equipment tiers, financing options, and the maintenance plan selected during the site visit.

Pricing reflects the manufacturer rebates and seasonal promotion active through the end of the quarter. Once the proposal is sent, the figures lock for 30 days so the homeowner can review without the numbers shifting underneath them.

Equipment tier — the recommended package pairs a variable-speed air handler with a high-efficiency condenser. A standard-efficiency alternative is listed below it so the homeowner can compare monthly operating cost against upfront price.

Financing — three terms are presented: a 12-month deferred-interest plan, a 60-month fixed at the promotional rate, and a 120-month option for the full-system replacement. The estimated monthly payment updates with the selected tier.

Labor and installation are quoted as a single line so the homeowner sees one number rather than a parts breakdown. The crew size and expected duration are noted for scheduling.

Permits and inspection fees follow the local jurisdiction's schedule. Where a permit cost can't be confirmed before the visit, it is shown as an allowance and reconciled on the final invoice.

The maintenance plan covers two seasonal tune-ups per year, priority scheduling, and a discount on any non-covered repairs. It can be removed without affecting the equipment warranty.

Warranty terms cover parts for ten years and labor for two, contingent on registration within 60 days of install. Registration is handled by the office once the job closes.

Removal and disposal of the existing system is included. Any refrigerant recovery required by code is performed by a certified technician and documented on the completion report.

A change to any line after sending generates a new version the homeowner must re-acknowledge, so the signed copy always matches what was presented.

Scroll back up — the footer's shadow returns as soon as there is content below it again, and the header's clears once you reach the top.

Modal preview

Width and scroll come from the controls below.

This is the modal body. Set the width tier and toggle long content from the playground controls, then reopen to compare. Head and foot stay pinned; only this region scrolls.

With long content on, the body overflows and the scroll-edge shadows kick in — a hairline under the header once you scroll, and a lift above the footer while there's more below.

Width tiers are chosen from content shape, not a one-off pixel value: Small for confirms, Default for standard forms, Wide for two-column layouts, Large for dense tables, Full for immersive multi-step flows.

On mobile every tier docks to the bottom as a sheet; the height tiers (half, tall, full) decide how much of the screen it claims.

Keep one primary action in the footer, on the right. Everything else stays a secondary or neutral button so the focal action is unambiguous.

Don't stack modals, and don't put a multi-step form in one — reach for a sheet when the task is exploratory or the content is long-lived.
