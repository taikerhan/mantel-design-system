Loaders

One family for every wait — pick by what you know about the load

Spinner

.mt-spinnerShort indeterminate waits · inline & in buttons
Loading…
14 / 18 / 28px plus a muted grey for neutral surfaces. Spins at a constant 0.7s linear — steady speed reads as "working". Use for waits a bare spinner can carry (under ~4s)..is-sm / .is-lg / .is-muted

Loading dots

.mt-dotsInline "thinking" / streaming — lighter than a spinner
Assistant is typing
Three dots pulse in sequence — scale, not a flash, so they stay visible. For chat, AI streaming, or any inline spot where a spinner would feel heavy..is-lg / .is-muted

Linear progress

.mt-progressDeterminate when you can measure it · indeterminate when you can't
Uploading 3 files72%
Syncing — time unknown
Determinate fills to a known percent with a tabular-nums label. Indeterminate (.is-indeterminate) slides a segment for background work with no measurable end..mt-progress-bar · .is-indeterminate

Circular progress

.mt-ringDeterminate in a compact footprint — tiles, stats
68% 24% 95%
SVG ring — a rounded brand stroke on an ink-150 track, percent centered in tabular-nums. Drive it by setting stroke-dashoffset to 100 − percent..mt-ring-track / .mt-ring-fill / .mt-ring-label

Skeleton

.mt-skeletonContent-shaped shimmer when you know the layout
Text run: title bar + paragraph lines of varying width.is-title / .is-text
Table-row loading state — circle avatar + two text lines + right-aligned chip.is-circle / .is-text

Rotating messages

.mt-loader · .mt-loader-msgLong async loads — cycle reassuring status lines
Crunching the latest comps… Pulling 14 months of pipeline… Formatting your proposal… Almost there — finalizing the numbers…
For loads longer than a bare spinner can carry, rotate short status lines so the wait reads as intentional. Each line crossfades + slides every ~2.5s — pure CSS, no JS — and honors prefers-reduced-motion. Pair with a shimmer skeleton for the incoming content..mt-loader · ~2.5s

When to use

Match the loader to what you know about the wait
Loader Reach for it when
Spinner A short, indeterminate wait (under ~4s) — inline, or inside a button while an action commits.
Loading dots An inline "thinking" or streaming state — chat, AI responses — where a spinner would feel heavy.
Linear progress You can measure the task — uploads, multi-step jobs — so show the percent. Switch to .is-indeterminate for background work with no measurable end.
Circular progress A determinate value in a compact footprint — a tile, a stat, a card corner — where a full-width bar won't fit.
Skeleton You know the shape of what's loading — lists, cards, table rows — so hold the layout with shimmer placeholders.
Rotating messages A long async load that would outlast a bare spinner — reassure with short status lines that cycle.
The deciding question is always the same: do you know how long, and do you know the shape of what's coming?
